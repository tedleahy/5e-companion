Character sheet, first pass

Navigation is a persistent header with the tabs attached underneath it. Spell details are sheet led, one card per way to cast. Both were picked on 12 September 2026 from two drawn options, and the dropped options are gone from this file. The plan doc keeps the record.

Brenna Duskhollow, level 6 Fiend warlock, is the example. She carries Pact magic, a second casting stat block from a Magic initiate feat, an attuned Ring of spell storing, and concentration running on Hex. Cure wounds reaches her twice, through the feat and through the ring, so its detail screen has two cards.

The header has two sizes, tall and a one-line bar. It should animate between them as you scroll, shrinking continuously rather than snapping. That is the one place the system allows motion, and it is not implemented here. Frames 2 and 3 show the shrunk state.

The first section also has desktop frames at 1440 by 900, each after its phone frame. The plate stays tall, Play and Stats sit side by side with Spells, Gear and Character tabbed in a third column, and details open in a side panel over that column. The scrolled frame has no desktop version, because the plate never shrinks. Tablet frames follow each desktop frame, trying the sheet in two columns.

The second section is Alarra Vane, a Life cleric. She prepares spells, which a warlock cannot show, and two of her values are replaced by the player, which introduces the override badge. She has the same desktop and tablet frames. Prepare spells opens in the side panel, with Save on its bottom edge. The Spells tab has no desktop pair, because the prepared list already sits in the first desktop frame.

The third section covers resting, gear, the character tab and editing a section, across both characters. Tabs carry icons above their labels.

The fourth section holds the detail screens behind the chevrons: items, features, a skill and the common actions.

The fifth section covers list editing, adding and removing, the money pad, and the remaining detail shapes.

The sixth section covers adding spells, proficiencies and languages.

The seventh section covers adding a feature from the catalog and the screens the sheet menu opens.

Every control answers taps across at least 44 by 44, with an invisible extension where it looks smaller. Show hit areas in the ink bar paints them.

The inks still mix. Nothing below uses a raw hex value.
