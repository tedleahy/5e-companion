Design notes

Rail: 2 items → 1
“Custom Subclasses” and “All Spells” are removed as standalone rail entries. A single 📚 Compendium item replaces them and becomes the entry point for all 8 content types.
No SRD/custom toggle
Lists show SRD and custom entries merged, exactly like the existing subclass list (isCustom flag). Custom rows get edit/delete icons; SRD rows don't. No extra filter control needed.
One hub, 8 types
Only Classes is fully mocked here as the representative type. Other hub cards are shown but dimmed — same manager/row/sheet pattern applies to each.
Reused, not reinvented
Row layout, expand transition, feature cards, and the create/edit bottom sheet are carried over from SubclassManagerCard / CustomSubclassFormSheet almost unchanged.
Try it
Click a hub card → Classes list. Click a row to expand features. Click ✎ / 🗑 on a custom row. Click + for the create sheet.
